The model is rarely the hard part. The hard part is the scorer: deciding what "correct" means for a document class or a label before looking at the output, separating a refusal from a miss, and keeping a measurement honest when the data is small, skewed or synthetic. These notes are about that layer — evaluation sets, abstention, cost per decision, and the results that disproved the hypothesis they were meant to confirm.
