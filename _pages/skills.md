Staff-level work is less about listing tools and more about **judgment under constraints**. These are the domains I practice deeply.

Batch and stream ingestion, transformation and consumption, forward/reverse-ETL, lakehouse design, Lambda patterns, data mesh where it earns its keep, modeling, contracts, schema evolution, SCD 1/2/3, quality, lineage, governance, cataloging.

**Engines:** Spark, PySpark, Databricks, Delta Lake, Iceberg, dbt, Hadoop/Hive where inherited.

## Streaming & orchestration

Kafka, Flink, Kinesis, Beam, Airflow / Cloud Composer, Prefect, CDC (Debezium / Datastream).

Google-ADK, LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, Haystack, OpenAI Agents SDK, Semantic Kernel, DSPy, RAG, fine-tuning, MCP, ReAct, prompt/context engineering, guardrails and AI governance.

**Vectors:** Milvus, Pinecone, pgvector, Chroma, FAISS, Weaviate.

HA, replication, sharding, backup/DR & PITR, tuning, RBAC, upgrades — treated as production systems, not side quests.
